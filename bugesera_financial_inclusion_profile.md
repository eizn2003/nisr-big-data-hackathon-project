## Why people without a bank account say they have none

Base: 437 respondents without an account in this district (weighted shares).

- No need or too little income: 83.2%
- Cost: 5.8%
- Alternatives & product fit: 4.2%
- Knowledge & paperwork: 2.6%
- Access: 2.1%
- Other: 1.3%
- Trust & permission: 0.8%

## Read with care

- Margins assume simple random sampling; the survey's design information is not available, so true margins are larger. Differences smaller than the margin should not be treated as real.
- Differences describe gaps; they do not explain causes.
- Mobile-money figures count registered users who describe themselves as current users, so they are lower than the headline for registered wallets.
