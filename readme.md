### Data requirements and variables

## Geography:

1. a1 = Province
2. a2 = District
3. a3 = Sector

## urban/rural classification:

1. a6=Residence area
   1. 1 = urban
   2. 2 = rural

## Financial inclusion:

1. Dimensions:
   1. access
   2. usage
   3. product
   4. quality
   5. impact on welfare
2. key indicator:
   1. the access strand

## Four mutually exclussive categories:

    1. banked=22%
    2. Formal non-bank only=70%
    3. informal only=4%
    4. Excluded=4%

## Financial products:

    **Bank**
    1. K1 = bank account in own name 1/2
    2. k2 = Anyone in the household have a bank account? 1/2
    3. k3 = Use someone else's bank account 1/2
    **Savings**
    1. g3a rows 1, 3, 4, 9(bank, MFI/non-Umurenge SACCO, Mobile Money saving, Umurenge SACCO)
    2. g3a rows 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11(education insurance, Ejo Heza, RNIT)
    3. g3a rows 12 to 16(group/ikibina,someone in the community or household, buying goods to sell,secrete place at home)
    4. g3a row 18 (not saving)
    5. g11a, g11b (pension contribution, old-age savings)
    6. g9 (Reasons for not saving)
    
    **Money management (Borrowing)
    1. h2a (borrowing money or goods from instituation or a person with a year)
    2. h3 row 1 (Bank credit)
    3. h3 rows 2 to 6 (digital loan, MFI/non-umurenge SACCO, NDFI, mobile money credit, umurenge SACCO)
    4. h3 rows 8 to 12, 15 (informal credit)
    5. h3 row 13 (Family and friends)
    6. h3 row 95 (Not borrowing)
    7. h7 (Main reasons for not borrowing)
    8. h8a, h8b (refused loan by institution and why)

    **Mobile Money**
    1. f4a = SIM card registered in own name 1/2
    2. f5 = About mobile money experience
    3. f6 = Number of Mobile money

    **Informal groups**
    1. l1 = Belong to any financial group, 1 = yes, 2 = No
    2. l2 = does it offer financial services 1/2

    **Insurance and risk**
    1. I6 rows 12, 15.1,15.2,14a (own insurance)
    2. I6 rows 13,14,16 (RSSB,private pension, Ejo heza)
    3. I5b (covered by someone else's insurance)
    4. I7 (reasons for not having insurance)

    **Remmittances**
    1.J1a, J1b, J3a, J3b (sent or received money)

    **Distance to reach financial products**
    1. D1a7,D1a8, D1a10, D1a11, D1a13, D1a14, D1a15, and D1b*  ( distance/time to reach financial offices or products)


## Demographics:

    1. b1 = Age
    2. b2 = sex
    3. hh_size = household size
    4. hh_head = sex of household head
    5. c4a = education
    6. c4d = disability
    7. c4b = marital status
